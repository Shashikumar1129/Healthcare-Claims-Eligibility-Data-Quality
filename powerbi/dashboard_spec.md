# Power BI Dashboard Specification

## Page 1 — Executive Data Quality
Records evaluated, exception rate, critical-field validity, critical exceptions, source volume variance.

## Page 2 — Eligibility & Enrollment
Status distribution, enrollment starts, plan mix, coverage anomalies, duplicate rate.

## Page 3 — Exception Analysis
Exceptions by rule, severity, source, plan and recurring failure reason.

## Page 4 — Claims Reconciliation
Orphan claims, coverage mismatches, claim status and claim amount associated with exceptions.

## Page 5 — Pipeline Monitoring
Expected vs received records, batch QA status, anomaly trend and before/after remediation.

Recommended slicers: plan, status, source file, severity, QA rule, coverage month and service month.
